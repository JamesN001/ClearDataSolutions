Remote Support Model

Support that comes to you,
without the travel.

Our solutions are built around remote delivery. It is faster for you, keeps costs down, and means help is a message away, wherever your business is based.

  • Faster responseNo travel time. Most issues are diagnosed and resolved in a single remote session.
  • Secure by designSessions happen with your knowledge and permission, and you can see what we are doing.
  • Location independentWebsites, dashboards, integrations and email support delivered to clients anywhere.
  • On-site when it countsHardware faults and installations that need hands-on attention are handled by a call-out.

On-site call-outs

Charged per km to your premises

If an issue cannot be solved remotely, we travel to you. Call-outs are billed by the kilometres travelled to your premises. The distance and cost are confirmed with you before we set off.
